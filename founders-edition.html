Founders Edition offer: closed beta rules

Leia em português

Effective: November 3, 2026. Run by MeuRobo, Brazil. Contact: oi@meurobo.app

What you can get

The Founders Edition skin pack for Saurora on Google Play, free. It holds 10 skins: Founder T. rex, Founder Ridgeback, Founder Deinonychus, Founder Scaled, Founder Dryosaurus, Founder Triceratops, Founder Pteranodon, Founder Kaprosuchus, Founder Tusked and Founder Pachycephalosaurus.

Its planned store price at launch is US$39.99. The price in your currency is set on Google Play and may differ. The Founders Edition is only sold for a limited time; after that it is never sold again. The pack has no cash value, can't be exchanged for money and can't be transferred.

Who can take part

  • You are 16 or older.
  • You have a Google account and an Android 8.0 or newer 64-bit phone, in a country where the closed test is available on Google Play.
  • This offer is for the Google Play version only. It doesn't cover iPhone or PC.
  • One pack per person and per Google account.

How to take part

  1. Join the group: groups.google.com/g/saurora-closed-beta.
  2. Become a tester through the opt-in link in the group's welcome note, then install Saurora from Google Play with the same Google account.
  3. Stay in the group and opted in for at least 14 days in a row.
  4. After day 14, email oi@meurobo.app with the word FOUNDER and the Google address you test with.

No purchase, rating or review is needed. Honest feedback is welcome, and it never affects the offer.

The first 100

The pack goes to the first 100 people whose valid claim email reaches us, in the order received. There is no draw. We check the date you joined the group. We can refuse a claim that is sent too early, repeated, made with a fake account, or from someone who has left the test. If you are too late, we will tell you.

Getting the pack

When Saurora launches publicly on Google Play, we email a Google Play promo code to the address you claimed with. Redeem it with that Google account before the end date in the email. No launch date is promised. If Saurora does not launch on Google Play, no pack is delivered and nothing is owed instead.

The closed beta

Saurora is free to test and unfinished: expect bugs, and possibly progress resets. It has combat, blood and online chat with other players. The community rules apply. You can leave at any time by leaving the group and the test on Google Play.

Your data

We use your Google address and your claim email only to run the test and this offer, and to answer you. Google runs the group and the test. See the privacy policy, and email us to have your claim deleted.

Other terms

This offer is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram or Meta, or Google. By taking part you release Instagram and Meta from any responsibility for it. It is void where the law doesn't allow it. We may correct these rules, and we keep the pack's promise for claims already accepted.
