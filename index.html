Every life begins small

You start small and hungry on an island full of things that would like to eat you. Find food and water, learn the land, and grow into an animal the others have to respect.

Choose your fights

A good ambush can matter as much as size, and one bad fight can end a life you worked hard to grow. When you die, that life is gone and you start again from the beginning.

An island worth getting lost on

Four kilometres by four of forest, river, marsh and open coast. The weather and the light keep changing, and there's always a safer place to rest, if you can reach it.

Not everyone out there is a dinosaur

In Humans vs Dinos, people land from a carrier with guns, trucks and helicopters, and they have a job to finish before they get out. Pick a side.

Choose your animal

Seven animals, each with its own way of staying alive.

T. rex

The biggest hunter on land, and it still has to pick its fights.

Deinonychus

Alone it's a meal. In a pack it's everyone's problem.

Dryosaurus

A small plant-eater with long legs. Running is the plan, and usually the only one.

Triceratops

Horns, a frill and a bad temper. Few hunters want to start this fight.

Pteranodon

The whole island from above. Nothing on the ground can reach you until you land.

Kaprosuchus

It waits at the water's edge, then comes out fast.

Pachycephalosaurus

A skull like a battering ram, and a habit of settling things head-on.
