Saurora Community Rules

Effective: November 1, 2026 (rules version 1). Contact: oi@meurobo.app

Hunting, ambushes and fights are the game. Hurting real people is not.

You agree to these rules before you play online. They cover your player name, text chat, voice and reports.

Be decent

  • No harassment, threats, bullying or stalking.
  • No hate: nothing against anyone's race, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, sexuality or similar.
  • No sexual content. Never anything sexual involving minors, and no grooming.
  • No sharing anyone's private information.
  • No encouraging self-harm, real violence or crime.
  • No spam, scams or pretending to be staff. Never ask anyone for their recovery code or passwords.

Play fair

  • No cheats, hacks or bots.
  • Don't use bugs to hurt other players. Tell us about them: oi@meurobo.app.
  • Don't attack servers, and don't dodge a ban with a new account.
  • Servers can have their own rules on top of these. Read them before you join.

Report and block

  • Report: open chat > Players, pick the player, choose a reason (cheating, abusive chat, harassment or griefing, offensive name, other) and add a note. The server's admins get it with that player's last chat lines.
  • Block: chat > Players > Block. Their chat and voice stop reaching you.
  • For anything bigger, email oi@meurobo.app with the player name, the server, the time and what happened.
  • Don't send false reports.

What happens

Depending on how serious it is, admins can mute a player, kick them, or ban them from a server. We can ban an account from all of Saurora. Very serious abuse can mean an immediate ban, and illegal content can be passed to the authorities.

Servers we don't run are moderated by their owners. Think a ban was wrong? Ask that server's admins, or email us for a game-wide ban.

Age

Saurora is for players 16 and older.
