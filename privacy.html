Saurora Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Saurora is a dinosaur survival game made by MeuRobo, Brazil. MeuRobo is responsible for the data described here. Contact: oi@meurobo.app.

The short version

  • No email or password. The game makes an account on your device by itself.
  • To play online we need your player name, your account ID and your internet address.
  • Chat goes to the server and is logged there. Voice is passed on live and never saved.
  • Bug reports and automatic error reports help us fix the game. You can switch error reports off.
  • Google handles payments. We never see your card.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your player name, your animal and your messages. They can record what they see and hear.
  • Server operators. Some servers are run by us. Others are run by people who hold a server licence, and in test versions by players hosting a game themselves (on a PC or a phone). Every server sees your player name, IDs, internet address, chat and voice while you play, and keeps its own chat log, reports and bans. Servers we don't run follow their owner's own practices. Ask them, and us, if you have a request about one.
  • Google processes payments and app downloads under Google's privacy policy.
  • Unity Technologies receives the device statistics above. In test versions, a game hosted or joined by room code runs through Unity's Relay service: it carries the game's traffic between players, chat and voice included, and sees your internet address and an anonymous ID made for this.
  • Our hosting provider runs the computer the account service lives on (rented servers in Brazil). Some connections may pass through Cloudflare.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell personal data and don't use it for advertising.

Security

The account service uses encrypted connections (https). Game traffic between your device and a game server is not encrypted, including chat and voice. Don't share private information in chat. Your account key stays on your device.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the game (Settings > Game > Account > Delete my account) or see our deletion page: https://arodinho777.github.io/saurora/delete-account.html.
  • Switch off error reports, voice and the microphone at any time.
  • Block a player: chat > Players > Block. Their chat and voice stop reaching you.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to object: email oi@meurobo.app. If you live in Brazil, the LGPD gives you these rights; in the EU or UK, the GDPR. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

Never send us passwords, card numbers or identity documents.

Age

Saurora is meant for players 16 and older. It is not made for children. If you think a child has used it, email oi@meurobo.app and we will delete the account.

Data abroad

The account service and our servers may be in another country than yours (Brazil). Unity's Relay servers may be in other countries too. We protect your data the same way wherever it is.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the game when the change is important.
